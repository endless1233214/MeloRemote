## App name

MeloRemote

## Short description

Control your self-hosted Music Assistant players, queues, playlists, and volume.

## Full description

MeloRemote is a focused native remote for people who run Music Assistant at home.

Connect directly to your own self-hosted server, sign in with a long-lived token or account login, and control players, queues, playlists, artwork, playback, and volume from one focused app.

Features:

- Connect directly to self-hosted Music Assistant servers
- Sign in with a token or Music Assistant account
- See live now-playing, queue, and player updates
- Browse playlists and search your library
- Play, pause, skip, seek, shuffle, repeat, mute, and adjust volume
- Add, remove, reorder, or clear queue items
- Manage favorites, library items, and playlists
- Works with local-network, VPN, and other user-configured server routes

MeloRemote does not provide music or operate a streaming service. A separately installed and configured Music Assistant server is required.

MeloRemote is an unofficial client. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Music Assistant, the Open Home Foundation, or any related project.
